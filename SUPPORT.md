# Getting help

- **Something isn't working?** Check the [README](README.md) first — most first-run problems are missing
  Accessibility or Screen Recording permission (System Settings › Privacy & Security). Then open a
  [bug report](https://github.com/chenkahana/Edison/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- **Have an idea?** Open a [feature request](https://github.com/chenkahana/Edison/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **Found a security problem?** Don't open an issue; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

When you report a problem, Settings › Permissions & Support › Export Diagnostics writes a short text file with permission states and settings
(no clipboard content). Attach it if it helps, after reading it.
